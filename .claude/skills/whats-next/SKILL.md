---
name: whats-next
description: Use when Cooper asks what to work on, says he has a spare half hour, feels stuck or overwhelmed, or types /whats-next. Picks one small task from ROADMAP.md that fits his time and energy.
---

1. Read ROADMAP.md.
2. Ask (in one line) how much time he has and whether he feels like coding, writing, or something visual, unless he already said.
3. Pick **one** unticked item that fits. Prefer items that produce something visible today. If nothing fits, split a bigger item into a smaller first step and add that to the roadmap.
4. Say what the task is, why it matters in one sentence, and the first concrete step. Then offer to start.
5. Don't list every option. One good pick beats a menu.
